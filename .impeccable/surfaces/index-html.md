## Direction contract

THESIS: The landing page is one calculator with a MODE row: the visitor presses the mode that is theirs (BAC, Clasa a 9-a, Profesor, Test) and the display shows what that mode gives them and how to start. Refuses the category default of four pastel route cards in a row.

OWN-WORLD: The shared calculator world: graph-paper chassis ground, panel with hard 1px edges, keys with a bottom edge that sink when pressed (active mode lit blue), a large green-gray LCD (backlit in dark) with dot-matrix numerals, the navbar's calm blue.

STORY: A visitor reads the promise, sees the real bank size and the official barem claim on a display, presses their mode, reads three concrete lines about it, and starts (sign up as student, join the grade IX waitlist, sign up as teacher) or sees that the level test is coming.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left, the headline (two lines, second in blue) with one supporting sentence, the primary key "Începe pregătirea" and "Am deja cont". Right, a small display with the real figures (exercises from BM.EXERCISES, 12 tipuri de itemi, barem oficial). Below, the calculator: four MODE keys, then the mode's display and its action key.

FORM: Selectorul de moduri, position 6 on the ordered list (1 hero LCD + route keypad, 2 live barem demo, 3 split device illustration, 4 printout tape hero, 5 per-role scroll panels, 6 MODE selector, 7 exam sheet). Seed key c576cdbf. Signature interaction: pressing a MODE key sinks it, lights it, and the display refreshes into that mode (annunciator changes, lines print in one by one). Motion grammar: one load sequence (headline rises, display power-on segment test, keys land), stepped display refreshes, key press sink; reduced motion renders static.
