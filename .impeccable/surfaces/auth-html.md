# Autentificare (auth.html)

Scope: the sign-in / sign-up / password-reset page (three forms on one page, Google sign-in, role choice Elev or Profesor). Visitor mode: Operate with one Persuade moment (a new visitor sees what the site holds before creating an account). Audience: grade XII students on phones and laptops, teachers. Constraints: all behavior of js/auth-page.js stays (Supabase email/password, username mode, Google, reset, role, redirects), Romanian copy without em dashes, light and dark, any width from 320px, no new facts: the figures on the display come from BM.EXERCISES and BM.CATEGORIES, "3 ExamTokenuri" is the existing signup copy.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is the calculator powering on to a locked state: a display that reads out the real bank and talks you through each field, and a panel of keys and slots that opens it. Refuses the category default: brand-gradient half with a feature list of icon tiles, next to a big rounded white card.

OWN-WORLD: The shared calculator world. Left: a solid Cover-blue column (the navbar's blue, no gradient) holding the wordmark, the headline and a bezeled LCD; the Acasă link and theme switch are nav-style keys. Right: graph-paper ground with one chassis panel (1px edge, 3px corners, no shadow): mode keys, recessed slots for fields, a primary blue key on a 3px edge, a neutral Google key. Eight-cell segment counter under the password. Doto only on the LCD.

STORY: A visitor sees the bank is real (656 exercises, 4 chapters, read off the display), presses Conectare or Înregistrare, and is guided by the display: it names the focused field and prints its rule (username format, 8 characters, teacher approval). They finish in one pass with no feature pitch to read.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop 1440: left column 42% with the wordmark top-left, Acasă key top-right of the column, the two-line headline, one sentence, then the LCD (annunciators CONECTARE / ÎNREGISTRARE / RESETARE, the typed readout line, figure and unit). Right: the panel centered, mode keys on top, fields, primary key, "sau", Google key. Phone: a compact blue header (wordmark, theme key, one-line LCD) above the panel.

FORM: Ecran împărțit, LCD + formular (chosen by the user from three structures: split screen LCD + form, one centered panel, printed access card). Signature interaction: the display answers the form: power-on segment test, the readout retypes when the mode changes, and it prints the rule of the field you are in; password cells light as you type. Seed key: none (surface inside an established world, no concept roll).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
